Covering criteria characterize relaxed robustness on semimetric spaces. For
finite totally ordered spaces, boundary criteria characterize triviality, and
every $(\mu,\varepsilon)$-robust partition is trivial when
$\varepsilon\ge\Delta\delta\mu+\Delta$. Triviality transfers through finite
$\mu$-closed overlapping decompositions. Restriction and union admit
counterexamples. Canonical binary floats,
sum products, synchronized maximum products, and padded lists provide ordered
semimetric constructions.
